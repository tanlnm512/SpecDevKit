## Articles
- **C-01**: task.md is the only status holder — no status lives in any
  other file, tool, or conversation memory.
- **C-02**: every generated artifact is regenerate-only: one committed
  source of truth, regenerated by a tool, never hand-edited, with a
  mechanical drift check.
- **C-03**: the harness-neutral core (scripts/, briefs, templates) must
  not grow harness-specific code; per-harness surface is a generated
  dialect living under tools/ or the skill's own dialect dirs.
- **C-04**: reuse before writing — no new abstraction with a single
  caller.
- **C-05**: decisions are append-only (D-###); nothing rewrites a recorded
  decision, and every divergence from a doc contract gets its own D-###.
- **C-06**: stdlib-only Python, no new runtime dependency without a
  tech-spec D-### decision.

## Rationale
- C-01: a second status surface inevitably disagrees with the first;
  resume correctness depends on one truth.
- C-02: two representations of the same fact drift; the repo's own
  decisions/007 exists because of this.
- C-03: harness APIs inside the core rot the core for every other
  harness (D-015's whole model).
- C-04: single-caller abstractions cost more than the duplication they
  remove.
- C-05: silent rewrites of decisions launder "what happened" into "what I
  wish had happened".
- C-06: zero-install stdlib tooling is part of the repo's contract with
  its users.
